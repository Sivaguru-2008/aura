# AURA — What It Is, Why It Doesn't Exist Yet, and How the Whole Thing Works

**AURA** — *Adaptive Uncertainty‑aware Reasoning Assistant*
> *"The clinical copilot that knows what it doesn't know."*

---

## 1. What AURA is (in one paragraph)

AURA is a **fully offline, privacy‑preserving clinical reasoning platform**. You give it a
medical study (today: a chest X‑ray or a brain MRI), and instead of just handing back a
label like *"Pneumonia"*, it returns a **calibrated, evidence‑grounded, uncertainty‑aware
second opinion**: how confident it actually is, which evidence supports or contradicts the
call, what's *missing*, which test to run next for the most diagnostic value per dollar, and
— crucially — an explicit, automated license to say **"I don't know"** rather than fail
silently. No cloud, no API keys, no patient data ever leaving the machine.

The product is not the diagnosis. **The product is calibrated doubt.**

---

## 2. What doesn't exist in the market today

Most medical AI on the market is a **perception model**: a classifier that takes pixels and
emits static probabilities. It is confidently wrong in exactly the situations where a
clinician most needs a warning. The gaps AURA is built to close:

| The market gives you… | The gap | What AURA brings |
|---|---|---|
| A label ("Pneumonia: 0.83") | The 0.83 is usually **miscalibrated** — softmax confidence ≠ real probability | **Temperature‑scaled + conformal** outputs with a *measured* calibration guarantee (ECE reported, not asserted) |
| One number, no bounds | No statistical coverage guarantee on the prediction | **Mondrian (class‑conditional) conformal prediction sets** + **online Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI)** that self‑corrects coverage from confirmed outcomes |
| Always answers | It **guesses** even on garbage / out‑of‑distribution input | **Energy‑score OOD detection + an explicit abstention policy** — the model is allowed to refuse |
| A heatmap, maybe | No traceability from conclusion back to evidence | **Every sentence in the report traces to the evidence nodes that produced it** |
| "Here's your answer, goodbye" | No help on *what to do next* | **Missing‑evidence engine** ranks the next diagnostic step by expected information gain per unit cost & risk |
| Cloud‑hosted, PHI leaves the box | Privacy / regulatory nightmare | **100% offline**, runs on a laptop CPU, no PHI egress |
| Black‑box confidence | Can't tell when a quantum/exotic claim is real vs. marketing | **Honest, reproducible benchmarking** — a built‑in fair fight between quantum and classical backends, numbers regenerated by a command, not claimed |

**The thing that genuinely doesn't exist elsewhere:** a medical AI whose *core deliverable*
is a trustworthy uncertainty estimate wired end‑to‑end into the served answer — with a
working conformal coverage guarantee, an abstention path, an information‑gain recommender,
and a self‑correcting feedback loop — all running offline. Everyone ships the classifier.
Almost nobody ships the **doubt discipline** around it.

## 3. What AURA brings — the headline capabilities

- **Calibrated doubt as a first‑class output.** Deep‑ensemble epistemic variance, temperature
  scaling, and conformal sets are computed on the served posterior — the impression a clinician
  reads always reflects the final *validated* distribution, not a raw logit.
- **A working conformal guarantee, online.** Class‑conditional conformal prediction sets with
  Adaptive Conformal Inference that updates coverage from confirmed clinical outcomes.
- **Refusal, not hallucination.** Energy‑based OOD detection + a conflict guard + an explicit
  abstention policy. Feed it a non‑chest image and it declines instead of inventing a lung
  finding.
- **Missing‑evidence recommender.** Ranks the next diagnostic step by **Expected Information
  Gain (EIG) per unit cost and risk** — turns "I'm uncertain" into "order a BNP next."
- **Explainability everywhere.** Grad‑CAM / Grad‑CAM++ / Integrated Gradients / SmoothGrad on
  the CNN (occlusion as a model‑agnostic cross‑check), plus Shapley‑style attribution and live
  counterfactuals over every evidence node — computed on the **full‑resolution** film.
- **Grounded reports + a learning loop.** Every sentence traces to its evidence; the
  clinician's accept / edit / reject verdicts feed the online ACI update with a full audit
  trail.
- **Quantum evidence fusion — placed honestly.** An 8‑qubit variational circuit fuses the
  evidence vector, with a classical Bayesian twin trained beside it and a **fair head‑to‑head
  benchmark** so the quantum claim is falsifiable (see §6).
- **A modality router.** A chest‑X‑ray product turned into a modular **Medical AI Operating
  System**: an upload arrives, the router figures out the study type, and dispatches to the
  right engine (Thorax live; NeuroMind/brain MRI now serving a real BraTS model).

### Stage‑by‑stage

1. **Intake & Modality Routing** (`backend/core/`). The upload passes an allowlist and size
   cap, is staged to a scratch area, and the router determines the modality (chest X‑ray vs.
   brain MRI; multi‑file NIfTI is routed via a declared `declared_modality` because it won't
   auto‑detect). Each modality maps to an independently replaceable **engine** via a plug‑in
   registry — adding CT, mammography, retina, or ultrasound is one engine class + one registry
   entry, not a rewrite.

2. **Vision Gate & Engine** (`services/vision/`). A structural gate validates the image
   (PNG/JPG/DICOM, VOI‑LUT, monochrome inversion, aspect rules) and — importantly — checks
   that a chest study is actually a chest study, so a brain MRI can't sail through and get
   labeled "Pneumonia." The engine runs a fine‑tuned **DenseNet‑121 at full 224×224** and
   outputs **7 sigmoid finding probabilities + a 1024‑d feature embedding**, each finding
   individually Platt‑calibrated.

3. **Evidence Encoding** (`services/fusion/evidence.py`). Findings plus structured clinical
   priors are mapped to an **8‑channel evidence vector** `x ∈ [0,1]^8` — the shared substrate
   both fusion backends consume.

4. **Evidence Fusion** (`services/fusion/engine.py`). `x` is evaluated by an **8‑qubit
   PennyLane Variational Quantum Circuit** (angle‑encoded, with entangling layers for
   higher‑order interactions) *and* a classical **Product‑of‑Experts** twin. A **Wasserstein
   Conflict Guard** detects when the two diverge and auto‑falls‑back to the trusted classical
   posterior.

5. **Clinical Reasoning** (`services/reasoning/engine.py`). Fusion output is combined with
   labs/symptom history (e.g., BNP, fever) via guideline‑derived log‑likelihood ratios —
   image evidence meets structured clinical context.

6. **Safety & Calibration** (`services/safety/engine.py`). The heart of the product:
   temperature‑scaled logits, **class‑conditional (Mondrian) conformal prediction sets**,
   **energy‑score OOD** detection, and an **explicit abstention policy**. Online **Adaptive
   Conformal Inference** keeps coverage honest as real outcomes come in.

7. **Explainability** (`services/explain/engine.py`). Grad‑CAM++ regional heatmaps,
   Integrated Gradients / SmoothGrad, occlusion as a model‑agnostic cross‑check, leave‑one‑out
   / Shapley‑style feature attribution, and live counterfactuals over every evidence node —
   all on the full‑resolution film.

8. **Missing‑Evidence Recommender** (`services/recommend/engine.py`). Ranks candidate next
   steps by **Expected Information Gain per unit cost and risk** — the "what should I do next"
   engine.

9. **Grounded Report & Memory** (`services/report/`, `services/memory/`). Composes a report
   where **every sentence is traceable to the evidence nodes** that produced it, and indexes
   the 1024‑d embeddings for longitudinal / similar‑case retrieval.

10. **Feedback Loop.** The clinician's accept / edit / reject verdicts feed the learning loop
    and the online ACI update, with a full audit trail — the system gets more calibrated the
    more it's used.

## 5. Architecture & how it's built

- **Modular, event‑driven.** Each engine is an independently replaceable package under
  [`aura/services/`](aura/services), speaking shared **Pydantic contracts**
  ([`aura/schemas/`](aura/schemas)) over a process‑wide async **event bus**. A **FastAPI
  gateway** ([`aura/gateway/`](aura/gateway)) orchestrates and persists.
- **The modality router** ([`aura/backend/`](aura/backend)) sits *alongside* the untouched
  chest‑X‑ray stack — the audited, calibrated Thorax pipeline keeps its paths and its audit
  trail; the router adapts over it rather than rewriting it.
- **Multi‑modal foundation layer** (`backend/foundation/`, `backend/vision/brain/`): real
  DICOM/NIfTI/NRRD readers, QC and standardisation, and a multi‑task BraTS brain engine — so
  brain MRI is no longer a placeholder but serves a real trained checkpoint.
- **Zero‑dependency web console** ([`aura/apps/web/`](aura/apps/web)). Entering `/app`
  collapses the interface into a particle core; the engines report in over the live gateway
  and the clinical console assembles itself.
- **Runs immediately.** Trained models ship in `aura/artifacts/`. Retrain from scratch in
  ~30 s on a laptop CPU.

## 6. The honesty layer (why AURA is trustworthy about itself)

AURA leads with **calibration discipline and a working conformal guarantee**, not an
unverifiable quantum claim.

**What the quantum layer is for.** Not a speed‑up — at 8 qubits this task is classically
simulable and no advantage is claimed. What it provides is a different *resource model*:
quantum precision is bought with measurements (`Var[⟨Z⟩] = (1 − ⟨Z⟩²)/n_shots`), which lets
AURA separate two kinds of "unsure" that every classical model conflates — **measurement‑
limited** (buy more shots; AURA says how many) versus **model‑limited** (tied at infinite
precision; escalate to a human). Over 447 studies: 86.4% commit, median 128 shots against a
fixed 512 budget, and of 61 abstentions **1 was measurement‑limited and 60 model‑limited**.
That capability is on the serving path, and it is the answer to "what did quantum buy you?"

**What it does not buy.** A built‑in, reproducible benchmark on real MIMIC‑CXR evidence, with
**each backend temperature‑scaled on its own calibration split** (a *fair* fight), shows the
VQC does **not** beat a fairly‑calibrated classical head:

| backend (real held‑out, n=69) | accuracy | ECE | macro AUROC |
|---|---|---|---|
| quantum VQC | 0.6377 | 0.2381 | 0.7696 |
| classical PoE | **0.6957** | 0.2194 | **0.7875** |
| learnable head | 0.6232 | **0.1879** | 0.7306 |

That is a four‑case difference at n=69 and is **not statistically resolvable** (McNemar
p ≥ 0.125 under every pairing) — so the honest reading is "comparable", not "classical wins".

```bash
py -m aura.aura_cli bench            # writes artifacts/benchmark.json
python ../audit_all.py          # DeLong / McNemar / bootstrap significance tests
```

The numbers above are **regenerated by those commands, not asserted** — and additional
research findings (e.g., that an entanglement ablation is a *negative* result, and that the
VQC once ran on a real IBM QPU with the top‑1 diagnosis surviving device noise) are documented
as measured facts, with failure modes disclosed rather than hidden. That self‑honesty is the
same discipline the clinical product is built on: **know what you don't know, and say so.**
